Layout:       load at $000A (the image covers $000A-$FFFF). Entry start = $0400.
              Vectors: NMI $FFFA, RESET $FFFC, IRQ/BRK $FFFE all into the test's traps.
              Feedback register at $BFFC: bit 0 drives IRQ, bit 1 drives NMI, 1 asserts.
              Success is `jmp *` at $06F5 (listing: "success ;if you get here everything went
              well"); any other self-loop is a failed trap. The later `jmp *` at $070F and $072C
              end the manual 65C02 WAI tests and are not reached on NMOS.
